# Welcome to the ContestDex!

Have you ever played Pokemon Emerald, Sapphire, or Ruby? Do you remember the contests? Don't feel bad if you don't! Most people overlooked it in favor of the Battle Frontier, and I don't blame them.

I, however, spent an extensive amount of time trying to become the ultimate Pokemon Coordinator!
Over the years, I gathered a large collection of bookmarked tabs in my browser that contained all sorts of contest information. Getting high ranks in the Contests involves a lot of berry collection, a lot of moveset adjustments, and a lot a lot of PokeBlocks.

Recently I decided that myself and fellow Coordinators needed a one stop shop for all relevant Contest information, and that's where the ContestDex comes in!

## The Pokemon Page
![PokePage screenshot](https://github.com/scarylyn/se_final-project/blob/stage-1-frontend-and-api/src/assets/screenshot-desktop-pokemon.png)
Here on the PokePage, you can filter by type to find the pokemon you want to run contests with. When you click a Pokemon, a popup appears with all the different moves that they can learn.

## The Moves Page
![MovePage screenshot](https://github.com/scarylyn/se_final-project/blob/stage-1-frontend-and-api/src/assets/screenshot-desktop-moves.png)
Here on the Moves Page, you can search moves by their contest type. When a move is clicked, a popup appears that tells you exactly that that move does within a contest (not within a battle, we don't care about that!), as well as which Pokemon can learn that move.

## The Berries Page
![BerryPage screenshot](https://github.com/scarylyn/se_final-project/blob/stage-1-frontend-and-api/src/assets/screenshot-desktop-berries.png)
The berries page is where you can filter all berries by flavors, and just like the other pages a popup appears when a berry is clicked. This popup will tell you what the berry does when it's eaten, as well as the potency of all flavors associated with that berry. By default the filters show every berry that has a potency level over 0 for whatever flavor you've chosen, in the popup you'll see exactly what those potency levels are.

## The Profile Page
![Profile screenshot](https://github.com/scarylyn/se_final-project/blob/stage-1-frontend-and-api/src/assets/screenshot-desktop-profile.png)
Finally, we have your profile page. This is where you'll see all your favorited items, for easier access to the information most relevant to you in your current game.

### My Future Plans
My main goal for deployment was functionality, so I feel like the page lacks visual personality. I'd love to mess around with styling and give it more OOMPH.
I'd love to add an entire section dedicated to PokeBlock recipes, I was restricted to the current options due to lack of PokeBlock inclusion in PokeAPI.
The ability to filter by game would be great, I only play Emerald but I know there are players who play Ruby/Sapphire, and the available pokemon are different depending on the game you play.
